# Research and Semantic QA

Read this file whenever a note contains factual claims.

## Research sequence

1. Resolve material ambiguity before searching deeply.
2. Search the exact concept and its likely synonyms.
3. Open the underlying sources; never cite search snippets.
4. Prefer a primary paper, standard, official documentation, or originating institution.
5. Add an independent authoritative source for every core claim when available.
6. When only one credible publisher group exists, add a specific `source_limitation` to that core claim instead of implying independent confirmation.
7. Record title, URL, publisher, publisher group, publication date when known, access date, and source type.
8. Map each factual page to one or more claim IDs. Map every claim to source IDs.
9. Preserve disagreements, scope limits, and uncertainty; do not blend them into a false consensus.

Two pages on one publisher's website are not independent publisher groups. A news article repeating a press release is not an independent confirmation of the underlying claim.

## High-risk topics

For medical, legal, financial, safety, or rapidly changing claims, browse by default and use the latest authoritative guidance. Add a bounded caveat and avoid prescriptive advice unless the user explicitly requests it and the sources support it.

## Final semantic review

Review the final rendered words rather than only the draft ledger. Check:

- names, dates, quantities, units, symbols, and version numbers;
- category errors, such as confusing a workflow with a model or a standard with an implementation;
- direction of causality and whether a correlation was overstated;
- universals and superlatives unsupported by the source;
- whether examples are clearly examples rather than definitions;
- whether edits introduced a claim absent from the ledger;
- whether title and caption remain accurate after simplification.

Mark the note failed when any factual sentence lacks traceability or conflicts with its source. Geometry tests cannot override this review.
